Email Spam Tester lets your hosting customers check how email from their domains is delivered, from the server itself.

A customer opens it from the Mail tab of a domain and sends a test email that leaves through your Postfix, signed with the domain's DKIM key, from its real IP and HELO name. The report covers authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), the sending server (reverse DNS, HELO, blocklists, TLS), spam filters and content, with a score out of 100 and the classic 0 to 10. With the folder check, copies go to test mailboxes at 14 providers and a table shows Inbox, Spam or Promotions for each.

Where the domain's DNS is on this server, DKIM, SPF and DMARC are fixed with a button, with the record before and after shown first and an undo. A History tab keeps every test with what changed. Optional constant monitoring copies the email a domain sends and reports on each new template.

Free. The extension talks to the Email Spam Tester API at email-spam-tester.com; INSTALL.md lists what is sent.
